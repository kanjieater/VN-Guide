This supplement records that pre-existing direction; the author is not self-granting a new exemption.

**Scope of the exemption:** Where no complete independent second Japanese written route guide could be found, use the directly inspected Japanese Shisnap walkthrough (https://shisnap.iza-yoi.net/game/pc/sayonara.htm) as the single detailed source for:
- The common opening and five heroine main-route choice sequences for Mutsuki, Mahiru, Nozomi, Miyuki and Koyori;
- The positions and reuse of its three documented saves;
- The documented day-9 heroine selections and their preceding visit/affinity paths;
- The named day-10/day-11 continuations and five heroine main endings.

For these exact **missing-independent-Set-B** facts, a genuinely undocumented `jpGuide2` may be `（第二ガイドに記載なし）`. This explicitly denotes **one-source support**, not independent corroboration. The first source's quoted text remains in `jpGuide1`; do not copy it into `jpGuide2` or invent quotations.

**What the approval does NOT waive:**
- All choices actually used must be present, in sequence, to form an executable route; do not insert invented or unsourced choices.
- Player-facing Japanese choice text must be exact where verifiable, not guessed from a truncated quotation. Preserve and flag any still-unverified full labels.
- The 2016-01-29 DLsite Windows edition (VJ009745) remains the exact `guide_target`. The original-edition chart's applicability to that edition must be honestly documented and reviewed. Single-source authorization is not evidence of edition parity.
- If independent written Japanese evidence exists for a specific fact, review and reconcile it. This exemption is not permission to ignore contradictions.
- Do not fabricate optional BAD END detours, affinity thresholds, new saves, or unlocks.
- Do not use videos, recordings, captions or non-Japanese derivative walkthroughs as Japanese verification evidence.
- Independent structural and accuracy reviews remain required. Only the accuracy stage/orchestrator may set `reviewed:true` following legitimate matching PASS records.

**Standards compatibility (resolved):** Shared-infrastructure PR #106, [merged 2026-11-04](https://github.com/kanjieater/VN-Guide/pull/106), amended the canonical source rules to honor owner-approved title-scoped exceptions for VNs.
